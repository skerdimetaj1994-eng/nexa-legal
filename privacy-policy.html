Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

NEXA respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use NEXA, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

  • Email address
  • Profile name
  • Age
  • Bio
  • City
  • Gender and relationship preferences
  • Interests and relationship intention
  • Profile visibility preference
  • Likes, passes, matches, blocks and reports
  • Messages exchanged with matches
  • Subscription or purchase status supplied by Google Play, where applicable

How we use information

We use information to provide account registration, authentication, profile discovery, filtering, matching, messaging, profile visibility controls, safety features, subscription features and account management.

Firebase services

NEXA uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for authentication and app data storage.

Payments

Where paid features are offered, payments are processed by Google Play. NEXA does not directly collect or store payment card details.

Profile visibility

You can control whether your profile appears in Discover using the Profile Visibility setting in NEXA.

Location information

NEXA does not display precise live location to other users.

Profile photos

Profile photo upload is not currently enabled in this version of NEXA. This policy will be updated if photo upload is enabled later.

Account deletion

You can delete your account inside NEXA at Profile → Privacy & Account → Delete Account.

If you cannot access the app, use the NEXA Account Deletion page.

Data deletion and retention

When account deletion is completed, NEXA is designed to delete associated account data including profile data, likes, passes, blocks, matches and chat data, except where limited retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes.

Adults only

NEXA is intended only for people aged 18 or older.

Contact

For privacy questions or account requests, contact Nexareview8@gmail.com. Never send your password by email.
