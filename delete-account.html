Delete Your NEXA Account

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Delete inside the app

Open NEXA and go to Profile → Privacy & Account → Delete Account.

Request deletion without access to the app

If you cannot access NEXA, email NEXA Support.

Email NEXA Support

Email: Nexareview8@gmail.com
Subject: NEXA Account Deletion Request

Include the email address associated with your NEXA account.

Do not send your password.

What is deleted

  • NEXA account
  • Profile information
  • Likes and passes
  • Block records
  • Matches
  • Chat data associated with the account deletion process

What may be retained

Limited information may be retained where necessary for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention or dispute resolution.
